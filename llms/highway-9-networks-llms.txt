# Highway 9 Networks

> Highway 9 Networks delivers AI Wireless Infrastructure as a Service — secure private mobile networks and deterministic 5G connectivity for enterprise AI workloads. Its Virtual Mobile/AI Cloud (VMC) platform pairs a cloud control plane, distributed edge running a 5G core, private 5G wireless, and connected device/robot fleets to give autonomous robots, cameras, and edge devices ultra-reliable, ultra-low-latency connectivity across manufacturing, warehousing, higher education, and energy. Backed by Mayfield.

## APIs

- [Highway 9 Mobile Center Streaming APIs](https://highway9.com/mobile-center-streaming-apis/): Streaming APIs for orchestration, telemetry, device management, alerting, and analytics on Highway 9 private mobile networks. Base URL https://partner.demo.highway9networks.com/api/v1/orch. Full specification available through the partner portal for signed partners.
